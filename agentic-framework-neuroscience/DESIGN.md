## How Triad uses this

The workspace keeps Krea's dark field, hairline borders, 16px body, and the gold accent `#ce9e33`. The sign-in headline uses the 60px / weight 400 ramp. In-app titles stay at the h2/h3 steps so the console stays usable. Primary actions are light on dark. Suisse Intl is the first face, with Inter as the available stand-in.
